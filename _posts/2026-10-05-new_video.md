Being in front of a camera is certainly a little different from my usual research activities!

As part of receiving the **Jülich Excellence Prize 2026**, I had the opportunity to talk about my research in a short video produced by Forschungszentrum Jülich.

<div class="research-video-section">
  <div class="research-video-text">
    <p>
      In the video, I share some insights into my work on
      plant roots, artificial intelligence, and computational
      modelling, and explain why understanding what happens
      beneath our feet is so important for agriculture.
    </p>
    <p>
      It was a great opportunity to look back on some of
      the questions that have kept me busy over the past
      years and to share a little of the science behind them.
    </p>
    <p>
      Many thanks to the team at Forschungszentrum Jülich
      for putting this together!
    </p>
