Being in front of a camera is certainly a little different from my usual research activities!

As part of receiving the **Jülich Excellence Prize 2026**, I had the opportunity to talk about my research in a short video produced by Forschungszentrum Jülich.

In the video, I share some insights into my work on plant roots, artificial intelligence, and computational modelling, and explain why understanding what happens beneath our feet is so important for agriculture.
